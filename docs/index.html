Record timelapses or regular video from your Mac's camera, with a clean Liquid Glass interface.

What it does

Timelapse or video

Flip the slider between a timelapse captured every 1 second to 5 minutes and a regular video.

Live preview

See exactly what's being recorded, with mirror and exposure controls and a last-frame thumbnail.

Keyboard friendly

⌘R to record, ⌘P to pause, ⌘, for settings.

Auto stop

Set a length in minutes and watch the progress bar until the recording ends on its own.

Keeps your Mac awake

Recording continues through long timelapses without the display or system sleeping.

Codec and quality

Choose resolution, HEVC or H.264, and quality, and pick where videos are saved.

Build it yourself

Requires macOS 14 or later and Xcode 15 or later.

./build.sh
open TimelapseCam.app
